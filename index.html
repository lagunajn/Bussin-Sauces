Our story

From Veracruz to Bussin'.

I have always been drawn to flavor — to the way a taste can capture a place, a culture, and a memory all at once. During my travels through Veracruz, Mexico, I experienced salsa macha in a way that stayed with me. It was smoky, layered, rich, and deeply satisfying.

That flavor connected with something in me. I wanted to share not just a sauce, but the feeling of discovering something bold and unforgettable. Bussin' is my way of bringing that experience home and sharing it with others.

Inspired by tradition and elevated with premium ingredients like olive oil, pistachios, pepitas, sesame seeds, dried cilantro, dried onion, and carefully selected chiles, Bussin' Salsa Macha is built to deliver crunch, smoke, and character in every bite.

“I didn't want to bring back just a flavor. I wanted to bottle the smoky, rich experience that made me fall in love with salsa macha.”

Every Bussin' jar is about more than heat. It is about depth, texture, and a premium experience that reflects the passion behind the recipe.
